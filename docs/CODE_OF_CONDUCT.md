# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project — filing issues, proposing
changes, reviewing code, discussing design — to be a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or
identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith before assuming bad faith.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussion or review.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
and any other official communication channel — and to anyone
representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer at
the contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be
reviewed and addressed as appropriate to the situation, which may
include a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban from project
spaces. Reporters' identities will be kept confidential.
